# Clarity and Conditions

The Clarity track, Mental Scars and the condition glossary.

## Clarity and Mental Scars

Clarity is a five-tier track recording lasting psychological and spiritual erosion; it moves rarely and memorably, and reaching Broken transforms a character rather than ending them. You called the draft good. (Draft)

**Structure (Draft):** acquisition is semi-structured (rules list qualifying events; the GM judges severity). Penalties are hard rules. Recovery is semi-structured: it needs significant time, safety and meaningful support, never a simple per-rest tick.

| Tier | State | Effect (Proposed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Clear | None |
| II | Uneasy | Narrative only: vivid dreams, unsettling impressions that may prove true |
| III | Shaken | The first fear effect each day lasts a round longer; slower recovery from mental conditions; some morale effects don't help |
| IV | Fractured | First failed Will save each encounter can't be rerolled; magical backlash worse; some creatures sense you. Benefit: you perceive hidden things |
| V | Broken | Gain a Mental Scar, then return to Fractured |

**GM test for progression:** will this experience fundamentally change how the character sees the world or themselves? If not, use a temporary condition (Frightened, Shaken, Dazed) instead.

**Qualifying events:** exposure to the unknowable; forbidden sorcery (Sorcerers most at risk); possession and spiritual corruption; unspeakable atrocities; prolonged Veil exposure; shattered convictions; accumulated trauma without recovery.

**Not qualifying on their own:** ordinary combat, corpses and bloodshed, common monsters, routine spellcasting, failed ordinary saves, mundane danger, natural disasters.

**Archetype interaction (Proposed):** Sorcerers flirt with the track constantly; Warlocks mostly avoid it, paying in blood; Hexers avoid it through caution; Veilwalkers may accept progression to see what others can't.

### Mental Scars (Draft)

Every Scar grants one benefit and one drawback. The player and GM choose the Scar that fits the event that broke the character; random tables are optional. Scars are permanent except by extraordinary story means. They must never mock or imitate real-world mental illness, and players are never punished for roleplaying deterioration.

| Scar | Benefit | Drawback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haunted | Once per Long Rest, GM reveals a hidden supernatural presence | Visions and whispers only you perceive |
| Obsessive | Bonus when pursuing your obsession | Ignoring it causes turmoil that sways decisions |
| Detached | Resist fear and emotional manipulation | Comfort, inspiration and morale effects work less well |
| Hypervigilant | Better at spotting ambushes, hidden foes, deception | Uneasy rest; trust comes hard |
| Veil-Touched | Sense spirits and weak points in reality early | Things beyond the Veil notice you more |
| Survivor's Burden | Extraordinary resolve protecting companions | Loss-related situations can overwhelm you |
| Hollow Mercy | Resist fear and intimidation from bloodshed | Dulled empathy; others find you cold |
| Echoes | Flashbacks can reveal clues to a current mystery | Similar sights or places trigger intrusive memories |

Archetype-specific scars (Arcane, Veil, Pact) were proposed for later.

## Conditions

Only the five elemental conditions have rules text; at least a dozen more are named in abilities and crits but undefined, and the condition system was queued right after Guard.

**Defined (Established effect, Draft durations)**

| Condition | Source | Effect | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burning | Fire | +1 fire damage at the start of each turn | 2 rounds or until an action douses it |
| Caustic | Acid | +1 acid damage at the start of each turn | 2 rounds or until washed off with an action |
| Frostbitten | Cold | Speed halved and no reactions. When it ends, you are Soaked (meltwater) | 3 rounds or until an action to warm up (changed 2026-10-16 from 1 minute) |
| Seized | Lightning | −2 to attack rolls | 1 round |
| Poisoned | Poison | −1 to all saving throws | 1 minute; stacks only with different poisons |
| Soaked | Water, rain, thawing frost (after Frostbitten) | Conductive: lightning damage against you +2 per hit. Fire damage against you −2 per hit (minimum 1), Burning can't take hold, and any fire damage ends Soaked in a burst of steam (Draft values) | Until the end of the encounter, until fire dries you, or until a Brief Rest |

**How conditions work (Established 2026-10-16):** a condition is Minor (Grit's Shake It Off can end most of them; positions and holds such as Prone and Grabbed end by standing or Wrestle instead) or Major (it can't). A condition applied by a critical hit lasts until the end of the target's next turn unless it says otherwise. The same condition doesn't stack; applying it again refreshes it (Bleeding is the exception). A condition that would hamper a task makes a skill test one tier harder. Effects are flat numbers or lost options, never advantage or disadvantage.

**Condition glossary (Established 2026-10-16; numbers Draft):**

| Condition | Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bleeding (1–3) | Special | At the end of each of your turns, lose HP equal to your Bleeding value; this bypasses Guard. Each new source adds +1, up to 3. Ends when bound: a successful Tend, a bandage, or a Brief Rest |
| Exposed | Minor | The next hit on you before the end of your next turn ignores your Guard and hits HP |
| Staggered | Minor | −2 Defense until the end of your next turn |
| Flat-Footed | Minor | No reactions until the end of your next turn (merges Off Balance) |
| Dazed | Minor | On your next turn you get only one of your Major or Minor actions, and no reactions until then |
| Frightened | Minor | While you can see the source: −2 to attacks, tests one tier harder, and you can't willingly move closer to it. The source sets the duration |
| Shaken | Minor | Clarity's condition, distinct from Frightened: the lingering unease of what you have seen. Effect to be written with Clarity |
| Slowed | Minor | Speed halved |
| Impaled | Minor | Speed 0 (the spear crit effect) |
| Prone | Minor | −2 Defense against melee and +2 against ranged attacks; your attacks take −2; you can't Dodge or Sprint. Standing costs half your Speed |
| Grabbed | Minor | Speed 0; you can't Sprint or Dodge. Ends if the grabber lets go, is Incapacitated or is moved out of reach, or if you break free with Wrestle |
| Restrained | Major | Grabbed, plus −2 to attacks and −2 Defense (nets, chains, bindings) |
| Sundered | Special | Guard maximum −2 until the encounter ends, stacking to −6 (replaces Sunder Guard) |
| Stunned | Major | Incapacitated until the end of your next turn, and −2 Defense |
| Incapacitated | State | No actions or reactions, and no Ward (used by Stunned, unconscious and Dying) |
| Hidden | State | Foes don't know where you are and must Search to find you. Your first attack from hiding gets +2, can't be Warded against, and reveals you |
| Fleet of Foot | Benefit | +10 ft Speed and ignore difficult terrain until the end of your next turn (granted by Tempo's Fleet Step) |
| Petrified | Major | Magic only; defined when magic is built |

Also in force: Surprised and Unaware (see Surprise) and the elemental conditions above (Burning, Caustic, Frostbitten, Seized, Poisoned, Soaked).

**Fatigue (Established 2026-10-16; merges Fatigue and Exhaustion):** 1 Weary, physical tests one tier harder; 2 Worn, Speed −10 ft; 3 Spent, −2 to attacks and Defense; 4 Collapsing, you fall unconscious. Recover one level per Short Rest and all levels on a Long Rest.

**Fatigue/Exhaustion (Open):** resolved 2026-10-16: one Fatigue ladder (above) replaces both terms.
