# Races

Playable races and subraces, with their attribute adjustments and traits.

Each race totals two +5 attribute bonuses and one −5 penalty, split across race and subrace (converted from +1/−1 on 2026-10-15 for the 0–100 scale); Dwarves and Elves are complete, Dragonborn mechanically done, Gnomes partial. Languages follow one rule: Common for all, the racial tongue only if raised in that culture. (Established)

### Dwarves

Once masters of the earth, the Dwarves dug too deep, unleashed something they could not control, and were abandoned by their gods. They now live among the ruins of their glory and guard the secret of their collapse to the grave, showing outsiders only polished facades.

- **Ability modifiers:** +5 Might, −5 Presence
- **Size:** 4–5 ft, \~150 lb, Medium. **Age:** adult at 50, live \~350 years. **Alignment:** tend lawful; caste-based societies.
- **Speed:** 25 ft, not reduced by heavy armor.
- **Darkvision:** 30 ft, no color in darkness.
- **Dwarven Resilience:** resistance to poison damage. No advantage on poison saves.
- **Stonecunning:** on Sagacity checks about the origin of stonework, add double your Sagacity modifier.

**Mountain Dwarves** (the ones outsiders meet; obsessive weaponsmiths)

- **Forge Master:** proficiency with Blacksmith's Tools. Armaments you craft during a Long Rest, for yourself or others, can carry **Tempered Edge** (+1 weapon damage), **Reinforced Frame** (armor grants DR 2), or **Counterweight Balance** (+2 to disarm attempts).
- **Durable Steel:** +5 Vigor.

**Hill Dwarves** (curious, practice many crafts)

- **Renaissance Craftsman:** proficiency with 3 artisan tools of your choice.
- **Hills Have Eyes:** +5 Acuity.

**Duergar** (lost Mountain Dwarves of the deep; forbidden craft, often for Dark Elves)

- **Eldritch Smith:** in a 1-hour ritual during a Long Rest with Alchemist's or Blacksmith's Tools, imbue an item you made or worked on: weapon +1 damage, or armor/shield +1 AC. Maintain 1 imbued item at level 1, 2 at level 6, 3 at level 11 (and you may imbue others' items), 4 at level 16 (bonuses become +2). Imbued items show unnatural runes detectable with Arcana or Occult.
- **Mind Smith:** +5 Sagacity.

### Elves

Ageless, secluded and indifferent to outsiders; with the gods gone, Elven rites honor ancestors and preserve memory.

- **Ability modifier:** +5 Acuity (Keen Senses).
- **Speed:** 30 ft. **Fey Ancestry:** magic cannot put you to sleep. **Trance:** 4 hours of meditation gives the benefit of a long sleep.

**High Elves** — proud scholars, most social of their kin. +5 Sagacity, −5 Presence (Arrogant Pride). 5'11"–6'2", live 200–400 years, lawful. Lowlight Sight 30 ft; 2 extra languages; proficiency with Calligrapher's Tools.

**Wood Elves** — rowdy tribal hunters, shy with outsiders. +5 Poise, −5 Might. 5'5"–5'10", live 400–700 years, chaotic. Speed 40 ft; darkvision 15 ft dark / 30 ft dim; Nature's Warden (basic communication with Beasts CR 1 or lower); resistance to poison damage.

**Dark Elves** — subterranean ritualists, presumed lawful evil. +5 Presence, −5 Vigor (Atrophy). Superior Darkvision 30 ft dark / 60 ft dim; Devil Sight 15 ft in magical darkness; *minor illusion* at will; Eldritch Mind (+2 to one Will save per Short Rest). Your original text framed Devil Sight, Misty Visions and Eldritch Mind as a choice of one invocation (Pact Masters); the later version grants all three.

**Rime Elves** — tundra warrior culture valuing strength and honor. +5 Vigor, −5 Sagacity. 6'0"–6'5", 190–250 lb, live 300–500 years, neutral good. Speed 30 ft; Brightsight (see 10 ft in blinding light even after failing the save); resistance to cold damage.

### Dragonborn

The Dragonborn of Asloria are a strong and hardy race. Their first appearance on the Plane dates back to the First Era, nearly 3,000 years ago. Though they inhabit their own sovereign nation, Hyrrokkin, many were displaced across the realm in the Third Era, whether by war or by independent endeavor, and they can now be found throughout Asloria. The sight of a Dragonborn is not impossible, but it is uncommon. With little exposure to such a monstrous race, humans are often wary and quick to act with prejudice.

- **Age:** Young Dragonborn grow quickly. They walk hours after hatching, reach the size and development of a 10-year-old human by age 3, and are adults by 15. They live to around 80.
- **Alignment:** Dragonborn of the Third Era lean toward no alignment in particular. Those living outside their kin's culture usually adopt the customs and alignments of their host nations.
- **Size:** Taller and heavier than humans, well over 6 feet and averaging almost 250 pounds. Your size is Medium.
- **Speed:** 30 feet.
- **Hardy:** +5 Vigor, −5 Poise.
- **Draconic Ancestry:** You are distantly related to a particular kind of dragon, chosen from the table below. It sets your breath weapon's damage and area and your resistance. Being of traditional Draconic stock, you also gain +5 Presence.
- **Breath Weapon:** As an action, exhale destructive energy in an area set by your ancestry. Every creature in the area makes the ancestry's saving throw, DC 10 + your Might + Vigor modifiers. On a failure, it takes 1d6 damage and suffers the damage type's condition; on a success, there is no effect. Damage rises to 2d6 at 6th level, 3d6 at 11th and 4d6 at 16th. Once used, it recharges after a Brief Rest or longer.
- **Damage Resistance:** Resistance to your ancestry's damage type.
- **Languages:** You read, speak and write Common, and speak Draconic if raised in Dragonborn culture.

There are two Dragonborn of extinct ancestry: Ferrous and Gem. They replace the ancestry table and its Presence bonus with the traits below.

**Ferrous Dragonborn** — Like their draconic ancestors, Ferrous Dragonborn are wildly resilient and deeply attuned to the metals of the Plane. Scales that glisten like cold, hard ferrous metal mark them. They did not inherit the mighty breath weapons of their ancestors, but something unique to Ferrous Dragons.

- **Iron Gut:** You can live on a normal diet and can also consume ferrous metals such as iron, nickel, steel and cobalt. After consuming a sword's worth of metal, you can expel it from your body in a gust as a breath weapon. Creatures make a Reflex save, DC 10 + your Might + Vigor modifiers, taking 2d6 piercing damage on a failure and half on a success.
- **Heart of Steel:** +1 HP each time you gain a level.
- **Magnetic:** You sense metal within 120 ft: its general direction and what metal it is, but not its exact location.
- **Iron Might:** +5 Might.

**Gem Dragonborn** — Gem Dragonborn are often outcasts, even within Dragonborn society, because their unnatural talent for manipulation drives others away. They look like any other Dragonborn until they use their psionic abilities.

- **Telekinetic:** You wield minor psionic ability: *mage hand* (invisible) at 1st level, *detect thoughts* at 6th, *suggestion* (up to two creatures) at 11th, *telekinesis* at 16th.
- **Mind Fortress:** Gem Dragonborn are naturally resilient to attacks on their minds. Twice per Short Rest, gain +2 to a Will saving throw.
- **Brains over Brawn:** +5 Sagacity.

**Open:** Iron Gut doesn't state the gust's area or how often it can be used. An earlier ChatGPT draft used a 15 ft cone, once per Brief Rest. Conditions are defined in the Conditions section.

### Gnomes

Cheerful small folk who keep away from other races. Adult around 40, live \~350 years; 3–4 ft, \~40 lb, Small; speed 25 ft; darkvision 30 ft; generally good.

- **Gnomish Innovation:** +5 Sagacity, −5 Might.
- **Gnome Cunning:** once per Short Rest, advantage on a Will save against magic. This conflicts with the minimize-advantage pillar.

**Forest Gnomes** (your draft): +5 Poise (Quick Footed); Illusionist (*minor illusion* at 1, *silent image* at 6, *major image* at 11, *mislead* at 16); Talk with Small Beasts.

**Rock Gnomes** and **Deep Gnomes** (Open): you asked for options and never chose. Proposed Rock Gnome options: Arcane Tinkering, Stubborn as Stone, Master of Mechanisms, Runes and Rivets, Crafted Memory, Durable Construction, Analytical Precision. Proposed Deep Gnome options: Shadowmeld, Deep Resilience, Burrow Sense.

### Hollowborn (shelved)

Mortals hollowed by divine punishment, feared as omens. Three subtypes were proposed: **Woken Hollow** (embrace the void), **Quiet Hollow** (suppress it), **Ravaged Hollow** (broken and lashing out), plus the traditions in the Setting section. All mechanics were written in raw 5e terms (Wisdom, proficiency, advantage) and would need conversion.
